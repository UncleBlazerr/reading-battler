Spell effect art (orb-*.png projectiles, boom-*.png explosion sprite sheets)

Original pixel art created for reading-battler — the fire/ice/lightning
projectile orbs and their impact explosions. Authored procedurally in
tools/gen-effects.py (Python/Pillow). Not derived from any third-party asset.

Style note: the chunky pixel fireball / explosion / lightning look was inspired
by reference images the project owner supplied, but the shapes, frames, and
palettes here are our own.
