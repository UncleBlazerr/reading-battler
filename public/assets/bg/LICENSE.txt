Castle room backgrounds (room-*.png) and the transition screen
(screen-transition.png)

Original pixel art created for reading-battler — the castle rooms the player
climbs through, plus the between-rooms loading screen. Authored procedurally in
tools/gen-backgrounds.py (Python/Pillow). Not derived from any third-party asset
pack or image.

Style note: the moody, limited-palette castle look was inspired by reference
images the project owner supplied, but the room layouts, decorations, palettes,
and every drawn element here are our own.
